Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why, and who handles it. I have tried to keep it short and plain.

Who I am

This website, nuvejo.com.au, is operated by Marco Linciano (ABN 51 498 278 849). In this policy, "Nuvejo", "I", "me" and "my" mean Marco Linciano. You can reach me at marco@nuvejo.com.au.

What I collect and why

The only personal information I ask for is what you type into the contact form:

  • your name
  • your business name (optional)
  • your email address
  • which package or topic your enquiry is about
  • your message

I use this only to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the services you have engaged me for. I do not sell or rent your personal information, and I do not use it for advertising.

Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages also receive technical information from your browser, such as your IP address and the pages you request. I do not use this to identify you.

Who handles your information

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts this website and delivers it through its network. The contact form is processed by a small function running on Cloudflare. The function does not save your message; it passes it on.
  • Brevo is the email delivery service that sends your message from the website to my inbox.
  • Google Workspace hosts my business mailbox (marco@nuvejo.com.au), where your message is received and stored.
  • Google Fonts. The pages load their fonts (Space Grotesk and Inter) from Google. When a page loads, your browser asks Google's servers for the font files, so Google receives technical information such as your IP address. Google's own privacy policy applies to that request.

These providers may process information on servers outside Australia. By submitting the contact form, you agree to your message being handled in this way.

Cookies and similar storage

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not use local storage or similar technology. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If this changes, I will update this page first.

How long I keep it

Enquiry details are kept for as long as needed to respond and for ordinary business records.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email me at marco@nuvejo.com.au or use the contact form, and I will respond directly.

Complaints

If you believe your privacy has been breached, please contact me first so I can try to put it right. If you are not satisfied with my response, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Google Maps and Google Analytics on websites I build

Websites I build for clients can include a Google Maps embed and Google Analytics, so visitors can find the business and the owner can see visitor reports. These are Google services. When a page loads them, Google may set cookies and process information such as the visitor's IP address, device and the pages viewed, and that processing can happen on servers outside Australia. Each client website has its own privacy policy covering this. This website (nuvejo.com.au) does not use Google Analytics or Google Maps.

Automated decision-making

This website does not use automated or AI-based systems to make decisions about you or your enquiry. If this changes, I will update this policy to say so.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
