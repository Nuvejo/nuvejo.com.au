Honestly

What I don't do.

Large online shops

A shop with stock levels, shipping classes and payment processing is its own craft, and not one I offer — there are specialised solutions like Shopify for that.

Photos and video

Good images come from someone who does that all day. I help with selecting and editing them.

Running paid ads

Google Ads and social media advertising are a job of their own with a budget of their own. I make sure your site is ready when the click arrives — running the ads I leave to specialists.

Promises of position 1

Nobody can guarantee a particular Google ranking, and anyone who says they can is lying to you. What I can tell you is what I do, why I do it, and what it realistically achieves.

Common questions

What most people ask first.

Why "from" and not a fixed price on the website?

Because a site for a painter with four services is a different job from one for a clinic with online bookings. The "from" disappears after our first conversation: you get a fixed number in the quote before I start, and that's the number that holds.

How does payment work?

After our chat you get a fixed quote. Once you accept, you pay a 30% deposit. The balance is due within 14 days of the final invoice, and your website goes live as soon as it's paid.

What happens if I want to change something after launch?

Your first month after launch is on me. Small changes - text, images, dates - are included (up to three rounds), and so is your first health report. After that you decide: carry on with the Website Partnership from $199 AUD a month, or pay for one-off changes by the hour at $75, always with the cost agreed beforehand.

Do I have to take the Website Partnership?

No, you don't. But honestly: hosting, SSL, security updates and backups don't disappear just because nobody thinks about them — right up until the day something goes down and nobody feels responsible. From $199 AUD a month I take all of it on, so you never have to think about it again.

Why a 6-month minimum term?

Because SEO and optimisation don't work overnight. I want to make sure you get the full benefit of the partnership — and don't give up after 4 weeks, before the work has even become visible. The first month after launch is complimentary and doesn't count towards the 6. After the 6 months you can cancel monthly.

What happens if I don't want the support any more?

After the first 6 months, you can cancel at the end of any month. Your domain and your website content stay yours.

Who owns the website in the end?

You do. The domain, content and code are all in your name.

I already have a website. Is it worth switching?

Absolutely. The web keeps moving — search optimisation, AI search, Google changing its guidelines constantly. A website that was good a few years ago is often technically out of date today. Send me the link, I'll have a look and tell you honestly what's worth doing. That costs you nothing.

How much work is this for me?

One conversation at the start, a few photos and some information about your services, and a few rounds of feedback on the designs (two in Starter, three in Business, five in Premium). Usually two to four hours all up, spread over several weeks. I do the rest.

Not sure which package fits?

Then don't work it out on your own. Tell me briefly what you've got in mind and I'll tell you which package I'd recommend — even if that's the cheapest one.
