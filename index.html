How I work

Five steps.
No surprises.

You know up front what happens, what it costs and when you'll hear from me next. The whole thing takes one to four weeks depending on the size of your project — and I'll tell you which end of that is realistic before we start.

  1. 01

    A first chat

    One conversation, about 20 minutes. I want to get to know you and your business — and what role your website should play in it from here on.

    Free & no obligation
  2. 02

    Design

    After our chat you get a fixed quote. Once you accept, you pay a 30% deposit. Then I design the first version of your home page — a real design, not a rough sketch. We go through it together, you give feedback, I adjust, and we refine it step by step until the design fits your business.

    30% deposit on accepting the quote
  3. 03

    Build

    I build every page individually: structure, words, images, forms, load time, mobile view. Along the way you get a preview link where you can check the current state at any time — including Sunday night, when something else occurs to you.

    Live preview for the whole build
  4. 04

    Launch

    Once the website is finished and the balance is paid, I handle the entire launch: domain, hosting, SSL, redirects and the settings that matter for Google. You don't have to deal with a single technical detail — I set it all up and make sure your website goes live cleanly. At the end, your site is up and everything works as planned.

    Balance due before launch
  5. 05

    Support

    Launch isn't where we stop working together. I look after updates, security, changes and the ongoing development of your website — so you can concentrate on your business.

    From $199 AUD a month
